1. Brainstorming Hobbies

Some interesting and easy-to-do hobbies for teens:

  • Reading books: Exploring new worlds through pages.
  • Drawing and Coloring: Expressing creativity with pencils and colors.
  • Playing a musical instrument: Relaxing with melodies.
  • Gardening: Growing small plants at home.
  • Cycling: Staying active outdoors.

2. Choose a Popular Hobby: Drawing

Why drawing is extremely popular among teenagers:

  • Stress Relief: Helps calm the mind after school hours.
  • Boosts Creativity: Enhances imagination and visual thinking.
  • Improves Focus: Trains patience and attention to detail.
  • Easy to start: You only need a piece of paper and a pencil!
