My Hobby — Drawing

Hello everyone, today I will talk about my hobby, which is drawing.

Drawing has been my favorite hobby for a long time. I usually draw whenever I have free time, especially after school or on weekends.

When I hold a pencil and a piece of paper, I feel relaxed and peaceful. It helps me forget all the stress and tiredness from studying. I love using different colors and shapes to express my feelings and create my own little world. Sometimes, I draw cute animals, beautiful landscapes, or portraits of my family and friends.

In the future, I hope I can improve my skills and draw even more wonderful pictures. Thank you for listening!
